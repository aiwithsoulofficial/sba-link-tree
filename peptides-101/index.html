So You're On a GLP-1... Now What?

They're incredible. Life-altering for many. But here's what your prescriber probably didn't tell you: a GLP-1 is one tool, not the whole toolbox.

That's where peptides come in.

The Basics

What even IS a peptide?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids - basically mini-proteins. Your body already makes thousands of them. They're the messengers that tell your cells what to do: heal this, grow that, calm down over there.

The ones we talk about tonight are either identical to ones your body makes, or synthetic versions built to do a specific job.

Straight Talk

Are they safe?

Most peptides are not FDA or TGA approved for these uses - they're classed as "research compounds." That doesn't mean dangerous, it means they haven't been through the billion-dollar approval process.

What it means for you: work with a peptide-literate practitioner, source from reputable compounders, understand what you're taking, and monitor. I don't sugarcoat - for each one I'll tell you the research, the risks, and what to watch for.

The Medication Itself

How Your GLP-1 Actually Works

Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and Tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) are not "willpower in a pen." They change the actual hormone signals that drive hunger.

The four levers

1. Your brain. GLP-1 receptors sit in your hunger control centre and create genuine fullness on smaller meals. This is the main driver.

2. The reward system. Receptors in your dopamine circuits quiet the "food noise" and cravings. This is also why they're being studied for addiction.

3. Your stomach. Food leaves more slowly, so fullness lasts - and this is where most of the nausea comes from.

4. Blood sugar. Insulin releases only when blood sugar is high, so they rarely cause lows on their own. Tirzepatide adds a second hormone (GIP) which is why it tends to do a little more.

On the horizon - Cagrilintide & the "triples"

The next wave (CagriSema = cagrilintide + semaglutide, and triple agonists like retatrutide) is showing 20-25%+ weight loss in trials by stacking extra hormone pathways. Not in Australia yet - but it shows where this is heading. For now, optimise the journey you're on. (Cagrilintide is in the catalogue below as Slim Beau.)

This Is The Exciting Part

Far Beyond Weight Loss

The science has moved fast in 2024-2025. GLP-1s are being reframed from "diet drugs" into broad cardio-metabolic and anti-inflammatory medicines. Here's where the evidence actually stands.

❤️

Heart & Cardiovascular Proven in trial

The SELECT trial (17,604 people) showed a 20% reduction in major cardiac events - heart attack, stroke, cardiovascular death - and the benefit started before major weight change. This earned semaglutide the first-ever heart indication for a weight-loss drug.

🔥

Chronic Inflammation Strong signal

Semaglutide lowers hs-CRP (a key inflammation marker) by roughly 20-40% - and it drops early, even in people who don't lose much weight. Much of the "systemic healing" promise rests here.

🧀

MCAS & Mast Cell Activation Early / theory

Mast cells appear to carry GLP-1 receptors, so activating them may calm the histamine-dumping response. A 2025 case series (47 patients) reported ~89% saw benefit - genuinely interesting, but this is uncontrolled, not a randomised trial. Promising, not proven.

🫒

Kidney, Liver & Sleep Apnea Proven in trial

Kidney disease events cut 24% (FLOW). Accelerated approval for fatty liver disease (ESSENCE). Tirzepatide became the first drug ever approved for obstructive sleep apnea (SURMOUNT-OSA).

🍷

Addiction, PCOS & Osteoarthritis Emerging

Because GLP-1 quiets the reward circuit, users show ~50% lower alcohol-use-disorder outcomes. In PCOS, early trials show lower testosterone and restored cycles. In knee osteoarthritis, weight loss brought a large drop in pain.

🧠

Alzheimer's - The Honesty Anchor Trial failed

Despite years of hype, the large Phase 3 EVOKE trials in early Alzheimer's did not work. The reminder that a beautiful mechanism doesn't always survive a rigorous trial - which keeps us honest about everything else.

Learn From Others' Mistakes

What To Watch Out For

🐌 Titrating too fast

The number one mistake. Rushing the dose ladder brings worse nausea, reflux, constipation and fatigue - with no faster results. When side effects show up, hold, don't climb. The lowest effective dose is the goal, not the highest.

🥬 Nausea & sulfur burps

Smaller portions, eat slowly, stop at first fullness, skip greasy food, ginger, hydrate, don't lie down after eating. Ease off sulfur-heavy foods for the burps. BPC-157 and KPV can help calm the gut through the adjustment.

👻 "Ozempic face"

Gaunt, aged look from losing fat too fast. The fix is rate-of-loss: go slower, prioritise protein, lift, hydrate. GHK-Cu and the GH stack support the skin and face through it.

⚠️ Gallbladder & dehydration

Rapid loss raises gallstone risk - severe upper-right belly pain after fatty meals is a "get it checked" symptom. And less appetite means you forget to drink: under-hydration worsens fatigue, headaches and constipation. Drink on purpose.

From My Own Journey

Plateaus Are Normal - Trust the Process

If the scale stalls, don't panic and don't quit. This is the single biggest reason people give up too soon.

What it actually looked like for me

I'd lose 3-5kg, then nothing for 2-3 months, then it would move again. Stalls and plateaus were completely normal for me - not a sign that anything was wrong. It's very much a trust the process journey, and the people who ride out the flat spots are the ones who get there.

The scale is a liar

Here's the wild part: in the months the scale didn't budge, my body was quietly reconfiguring. I'd drop a whole dress size while the number stayed exactly the same. Weight loss is a WEIRD thing - what's happening to your body is often way ahead of what the scale is telling you.

So measure differently

Don't live and die by the scale. Track how your clothes fit, take a monthly progress photo, and measure your waist and hips. Losing fat while the number holds is still winning - even when the scale is pretending nothing is happening.

1.2 - 1.6 g / kg / day

2-3x / week

Resistance train so what you lose is fat, not muscle. Creatine 3-5g/day supports strength and lean mass. The GH stack (FigureBoost) backs this up.

03

Electrolytes & Minerals

Eating less means fewer minerals and less food-water.

Na · K · Mg

A low-sodium, sugar-free electrolyte at least once a day. Add electrolytes - don't just chug plain water. My faves: Basica Activ and Ultima.

Don't lose your hair

Hair shedding is real, usually shows up 2-4 months in, triggered by rapid loss and nutrient shock - and is usually temporary. Protect it from day 1: hit your protein, check your iron/ferritin (low ferritin worsens shedding), plus zinc, vitamin D and B12, add collagen - and don't lose too fast.

Eat enough food

Appetite suppression makes it far too easy to under-eat - which tanks energy, accelerates muscle and hair loss, causes "Ozempic face" and stalls your metabolism. Aim for a moderate deficit, not starvation. Protein floor first, then enough real food, plus fibre for satiety and regularity.
